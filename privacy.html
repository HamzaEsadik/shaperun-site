ShapeRun

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

ShapeRun turns a shape you draw into a running route on real streets, records your run with GPS and lets you share the result. ShapeRun is built so that your data stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we run no servers that receive your data.

This policy is published by Hamza Esadik, the developer of ShapeRun, based in Morocco (“we”, “us”). Contact: hamza.esadik.m@gmail.com.

Summary

Data Where it goes
Drawings, planned routes, runs, GPS points, settings Only on your iPhone (and in your own iOS backups)
Your precise location Stays on your iPhone. Apple Maps shows it on the map as part of iOS.
An approximate area (about 5 × 5 km) around you Sent to an OpenStreetMap Overpass server to download street data
Purchase history and a random anonymous ID RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us

Data stored on your device

Your shapes, planned routes, completed runs (including every recorded GPS point, time, accuracy, speed and altitude), thumbnails, cached street data and your settings are stored in a database inside the app on your iPhone. We cannot see or access them.

Backups. Like other app data, this data is included in your iPhone’s iCloud Backup or computer backup if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under your iCloud or backup settings and Apple’s privacy policy. We have no access to them. To keep ShapeRun data out of iCloud Backup, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups and turn ShapeRun off.

Deleting your data. You can delete individual routes and runs in the app. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

Location

ShapeRun asks for location access “While Using the App”. It uses your location to:

  • find streets near you when you plan a route. If you have already allowed location, the street data for your area starts downloading as soon as you draw a shape, so the route search is ready sooner, and
  • record your run. While a run is being recorded, tracking continues in the background with the screen locked, and iOS shows a location indicator. Tracking stops when you finish the run.

Your precise location and your recorded runs are never sent to us or to any third party. Maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit), which is part of iOS and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Street data (OpenStreetMap / Overpass)

To fit your shape to real streets, ShapeRun downloads street data from OpenStreetMap through public Overpass API servers (currently overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee and maps.mail.ru, tried in that order). These servers are run by third parties, not by us.

To limit what is disclosed, ShapeRun never sends your exact location. It asks for whole grid squares of 0.05° × 0.05° (about 5 × 5 km), so the request only reveals roughly which area you are in. Like any internet request, the server also sees your IP address. Downloaded street data is cached on your iPhone for 30 days and reused, so the same area is not requested again in that time. The operators’ own policies apply to their server logs.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple)

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. as a data processor to validate purchases with Apple and to know whether ShapeRun Plus is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (products, prices, dates, renewals, refunds), a random app user ID that ShapeRun creates and that is not linked to your identity, and technical information needed to answer the request (app version, iOS version, App Store country, IP address). ShapeRun never sends RevenueCat your location, routes or runs. We use this data to provide your subscription and to see aggregate sales figures. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

When you choose to share

Share images and GPX files are created on your iPhone and only leave it when you send them through the iOS share sheet or save them to Files. If you use “Report a problem” on a route, ShapeRun opens a pre-filled email containing that route. It is only sent if you send it, and we then receive your email address and the message.

What we don’t do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of any data.

Crash reports and usage statistics may reach us through Apple only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings. They are aggregated by Apple and contain no personal information.

Children

ShapeRun is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you control it directly. For any question, or to ask for access to or deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat for your anonymous ID, email us. Depending on where you live (for example under Moroccan Law 09-08 or the EU GDPR), you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
