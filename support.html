ShapeRun

Draw it. Run it. Post it.

ShapeRun turns a shape you draw into a runnable route on the streets around you, guides you while you run it, and makes a share image of your GPS art.

Support

Questions, bugs or a route that sent you somewhere it shouldn’t? Email hamza.esadik.m@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. For a bad route, use Report a problem on the route in the app: it attaches the route for you.

Common questions

No route was found for my shape.

Try a longer or shorter distance, a simpler shape, or a preset. Shapes need enough streets around you, so dense neighbourhoods work best.

How do I restore ShapeRun Plus?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel Plus?

iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → ShapeRun, or in the app under Settings → Manage (on the Plus card).

Does tracking work with the screen locked?

Yes. Allow location “While Using the App”. Recording continues while a run is active and stops when you finish.
